This notice explains the cookies on techphase.net and how you can accept or reject them.

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how the site is used. It collects anonymous usage data, such as which pages are visited, a cookie identifier, device and browser details, and an approximate location derived from your IP address. How consent works

Pages that load Google Analytics show a banner with Accept and Reject. The two choices have equal prominence. Until you choose, and if you choose Reject, analytics storage stays off and these cookies are not set. Accept turns on analytics storage only. Advertising storage stays off either way.

Your choice is saved in local storage in this browser. That record is not a cookie. To change it, use Cookie settings in the footer. On this page, that link returns you to the homepage and opens the banner again.

You can also opt out by installing the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on, or by blocking and deleting cookies in your browser settings. Rejecting analytics, using the add-on, or clearing site data stops new analytics cookies. Google explains how it handles this data in Google's privacy policy.

Our contact forms and chat assistant do not set cookies. To display our website font, your browser connects to Google Fonts, which receives your IP address. See our Privacy Policy for the rest of how we handle personal data.
