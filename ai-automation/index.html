Process

How an automation project works

  1. Discovery call

    You walk us through how leads, bookings and admin flow today. We point out the workflows likely to save the most time.

  2. Workflow map and quote

    We write down the current process, the proposed automation, the tools involved and what a human still approves. Then you get a quote in USD.

  3. Build and test

    We build in your accounts (n8n cloud or self-hosted, Zapier, your CRM) and test with real examples before anything goes live.

  4. Launch with guardrails

    Anything customer-facing or financial keeps human approval until you're confident in it. Errors alert you instead of failing silently.

  5. Handover

    You get the workflows, written notes and a short video walkthrough. Everything runs in accounts you own.

FAQ

AI automation questions

What does an AI automation agency actually do?

We find repetitive steps in your business, such as replying to leads, booking, data entry and reporting, and connect your tools so those steps run automatically, using AI where it helps, like drafting replies or answering FAQs.

n8n or Zapier: which is better for a small business?

Zapier is quickest for simple workflows. n8n suits complex or high-volume workflows and can cost less at scale. We recommend one based on your volume and budget.

Do I need to change my CRM or tools?

Usually not. We build around the tools you already use.

Is my customer data safe?

Workflows run in accounts you own, and we only ask for the access each workflow needs. You can remove our access after handover.

Can you build an AI chatbot for my website?

Yes. It's trained on your content and rules, with a handoff to a human and clear limits on what it can promise.

Do you offer white-label AI automation for agencies?

Yes. We build under your brand and don't contact your clients. See our white-label page for agencies.
