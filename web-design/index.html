FAQ

Website questions

How much does a website cost for a small business?

It depends on page count, features and platform. A focused landing page costs far less than a multi-page site with booking or ecommerce. Send us your brief and we'll give you a quote in US dollars.

How long does a website take?

It depends on the number of pages, the features and how quickly content is ready. You get a timeline with your written scope before work starts.

Will my new site be good for SEO?

Every build includes technical SEO foundations: fast loading, clean structure, titles and meta descriptions, schema markup, a sitemap and Search Console setup. Ongoing SEO is available separately.

Can you redesign my site without losing Google rankings?

We map every old URL to its new one with 301 redirects and keep the content that already ranks, which is the standard way to protect rankings during a redesign.

Do I own the website?

Yes. The domain, hosting, code and design files are in your name once the work is paid for.

You're in Pakistan. How do we communicate?

Video calls during our weekday overlap with US Eastern mornings, plus email or Slack and recorded video walkthroughs. You can also call +1 (260) 239-4557.

What do you need from me to start?

Your logo, any brand guidelines, examples of sites you like, and the content you have. We can help write the rest.
