    Refund & Return Policy

    Last updated: October 2026

    Digital services

    Because our services are digital and custom-made, there are no physical returns. Refunds are handled as described below.

    Before work begins

    If you cancel before work starts, you may request a refund of your advance payment, minus any payment-processing fees.

    After work begins

    Completed milestones and work already delivered are non-refundable. A partial refund may be offered for undelivered portions, at our discretion and based on work completed.

    Quality issues

    If delivered work doesn't match the agreed scope, tell us within 7 days and we will revise it at no extra cost. If we can't resolve it, we will discuss a fair partial or full refund.

    Video & UGC content

    Once final video files are delivered and approved, they are non-refundable. Revisions are available within the agreed rounds.

    How to request

    Email info@techphase.net with your invoice number and reason. Approved refunds are processed within 7–10 business days via the original payment method.
