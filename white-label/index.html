Process

How the white-label process works

  1. Intro call and NDA

    We learn how your agency works, your quality bar and your tools. We sign an NDA first if you need one.

  2. Brief and scope

    You share the client brief. We send a written scope, a timeline and a quote in USD.

  3. Design and build

    We work on an unbranded staging link or in your tools, with regular async updates you can pass on to your client.

  4. Your review

    You review and request changes before anything reaches your client. Nothing goes live without your sign-off.

  5. Handover or launch

    We launch on your client's accounts or hand over files and access in the name you choose.

  6. Ongoing support

    Optional ongoing help for updates, fixes and new requests on the same terms.

FAQ

White-label questions

What is white-label web development?

White-label web development means a partner builds websites for your clients and you deliver them under your agency's name. Tech Phase does the design and development work, and your agency keeps the client relationship, the branding and the credit.

Will my clients know Tech Phase is involved?

Not unless you tell them. We deliver under your brand, with no Tech Phase credits, links or logos in the code, designs or files, and we only speak with your client if you ask us to.

Do you sign an NDA?

Yes. An NDA is available on request, and we can sign it before you share any client details.

What can you white-label for my agency?

Website design and development on WordPress, Shopify, Webflow or custom code, including landing pages, redesigns and online stores; AI automation with n8n, Zapier and AI chatbots; and UGC video ads, reels and promo edits for Meta, TikTok and YouTube.

How do we communicate across time zones?

Tech Phase is a Pakistan-based team. We overlap live with US Eastern mornings on weekdays for calls, work asynchronously outside that window by email or Slack with recorded video walkthroughs, and reply within one business day.

How does pricing work for agencies?

Partner pricing is available on request. Every project gets a written scope and a quote in US dollars before work starts, and larger projects are split into milestones, so you pay as work is delivered.

Who owns the finished work?

Your agency, or your client if you prefer. Code, designs, files and accounts are handed over in the name you choose once the work is paid for.
