Process

How white-label works with Tech Phase

  1. Brief

    Share the scope, any designs, the deadline and your client's must-haves. Written notes or a screen-recorded video both work.

  2. Written scope and quote

    You get a written scope, milestones, timeline and a quote in USD. You add your margin and quote your client.

  3. Kickoff in your tools

    We work in your Slack, ClickUp, Asana or Jira, and follow your naming and QA conventions.

  4. Build with regular updates

    Async progress updates and a staging link you can check any time. Live calls happen in our overlap with your morning.

  5. Review and handover

    Revisions within the agreed scope, then a clean handover: code in your repo, files in your Drive, accounts in your client's name.

Our rules

How we protect your agency

  • We don't contact your clients unless you ask us to.
  • No Tech Phase branding on deliverables, code comments, staging links or emails.
  • You own the work once it is paid for.
  • Scope changes are quoted first. If the brief changes, we tell you the cost before doing the extra work.
  • Honest about who we are. Tech Phase is a Pakistan-based sole proprietorship led by Tahir Ayub, and you will always know who is doing your work.
Time zones

Built around US hours

Pakistan is 9 hours ahead of US Eastern while the US is on daylight time, and 10 hours ahead from 1 November 2026 to 14 March 2027. We overlap live with US Eastern mornings on weekdays, and the rest of the work carries on while your team is offline, so a brief sent at lunch can have progress waiting the next morning.

FAQ

White-label questions agencies ask

Will my client know you're involved?

Not from us. We work under your brand, keep Tech Phase off every deliverable, and don't contact your clients unless you ask us to.

Can you work from our designs?

Yes. Send Figma or XD files, or even rough sketches. We can also design from scratch if you need it.

Which platforms do you build on?

WordPress, WooCommerce, Shopify, Webflow and custom code for websites and apps, and n8n, Zapier and AI chatbots for automation. Tell us your stack and we'll confirm if it's a fit.

How do you handle time zones?

We overlap live with US Eastern mornings on weekdays and work asynchronously the rest of the day, with written or video updates. Pakistan is 9 hours ahead of US Eastern while the US is on daylight time, and 10 hours ahead from 1 November 2026 to 14 March 2027.

How does pricing work?

Every project gets a written scope and a quote in US dollars before work starts. Send a brief through the form on this page to get a quote.

Are you a company or a freelancer?

Do you also do AI automation and web design for direct clients?
